Privacy at WeatherView

Updated November 4, 2026.

Saved places and device watches

Your saved locations, unit and outdoor-plan preferences, and device-only weather watches are stored in this browser. Watches do not create an account, send notifications or run while the browser is closed.

Location access

Location access starts only when you select “Use my location” and grant your browser's permission. Coordinates are sent to WeatherView to fetch the forecast. The browser also sends them to BigDataCloud to find the place name; that service receives your IP address with the request.

Radar maps

Radar tiles are requested directly from Environment Canada’s GeoMet service. Website map tiles come from OpenStreetMap; native maps use Apple Maps. These services receive your IP address and the map area you view when supplying the imagery.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits and use of features such as forecast tabs, saving a place, opening sharing saving a device watch, choosing plan preferences, viewing planning results, downloading a calendar plan and completing a share. These events do not include your coordinates, search text, saved place name or watch contents. Query parameters and fragments are removed from page URLs before they are sent to Vercel.

Vercel Web Analytics does not use analytics cookies. Its documentation explains the request information it processes and how it counts visitors. Hosting and weather providers also process requests needed to serve the site.

We also use PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how the site is used: it measures page views and clicks on the “Share this forecast” button, the “Free website widget” link and the “Find a time in the planner” link. Analytics data is processed in the US. IP addresses may be processed by PostHog.

PostHog events are sent only from the site’s public pages: the dashboard, city forecast pages, the city directory, outdoor planning, what’s new, the widget page, the weather guide and this page. A dashboard address can contain the place you chose, its coordinates and your plan settings, so before an event is sent, page and referrer addresses keep only standard campaign tags (such as utm_source) that are short plain labels; other query values and fragments are removed. The referrer is sent with the same filtering, and its path is kept.

The embedded weather widget does not load Vercel Web Analytics or PostHog.

Calendar plans

“Add to calendar” downloads a calendar file containing your chosen activity, city name, suggested time and forecast reasons. WeatherView does not read your calendar or save the event automatically. Review the file in your calendar app before saving it.

WeatherView for iPhone and iPad

The native app stores saved cities, preferences and the last forecast on your device. Searching sends the entered city or region to WeatherView's geocoding service. Search results use a local GeoNames place index and may be cached on our server for up to 24 hours by search term, without an account or device identifier. Forecast and planning requests send the selected city's coordinates to WeatherView and its weather providers. The native app does not request GPS permission, create accounts or include our website analytics (Vercel Web Analytics or PostHog). Hosting and providers process the network requests needed to supply data.

Public forecast data comes from MET Norway under CC BY 4.0, normalized by WeatherView. Visual Crossing forecast rows stay on our server and are used to calculate activity recommendations. GeoNames supplies the local place-search data under CC BY 4.0. These data are credited beside the relevant views.

Native radar uses Apple Maps with Environment Canada / NOAA imagery. Apple's calendar editor opens only when you choose a suggestion; you decide whether to save the event. WeatherView cannot read your existing calendar events. A shared forecast includes the chosen city and its coordinates.

Managing local data

Remove saved locations with their × button, stop a watch in its dialog, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Clearing site data also removes cached forecasts and preferences.
