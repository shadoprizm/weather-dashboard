# Place search dataset

Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities1000.zip, admin1CodesASCII.txt and countryInfo.txt. Retrieved October 13, 2026.

GeoNames data is licensed under CC BY 4.0; commercial use and redistribution are permitted. https://www.geonames.org/export/ and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

WeatherView normalizes populated-place coordinates, region, country, timezone, population and up to twelve alternate names into a compressed local search index. Search does not contact a third-party hosted API.
